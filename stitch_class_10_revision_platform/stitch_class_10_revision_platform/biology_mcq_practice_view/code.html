Which of the following is the correct pathway of oxygenated blood entering the heart from the lungs?

info

Concept Brief

Oxygenated blood from the lungs enters the heart through the pulmonary veins into the Left Atrium. It then passes through the bicuspid valve into the Left Ventricle, from where it is pumped to the rest of the body via the Aorta.
